Engineering outcome, 5 October

After the initial ticket submission, the course desk published an update on the challenge page: ticket #4471 closed on 5 October. The parser was fixed by fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. The hint was won’t fix because the booking-v3 form was retired. The two ticket closure reasons must preserve that difference: parser completed, hint not planned.

The exported reports and computed counts above remain the historical evidence through 4 October. Retirement is not evidence that the misleading hint was repaired, and this update does not independently explain each of the five anomalous reports. The fixes and checks discussed below describe the two failure mechanisms; the desk’s actual resolutions are the ones recorded here.

Shared parser: day-first dates read month first

For a day-first tenant, the shared parser reads the supplied date month first. The booking-v2 form, app and email paths fall in this group; booking-v3 app and email reports do too. For example, R-41200 reports 7 April becoming 4 July. Use the tenant directory and Intl.DateTimeFormat date-part order, not the reporter’s browser locale or intake tag, to identify the affected path.

Fix the parser to respect the declared tenant date order and use an unambiguous date representation at system boundaries. Test app, email and booking-v2 with a day-first tenant and ambiguous input such as 07/04/2026. Test a booking-v3 app report as well, because the form version alone does not decide the cause.

Booking-v3 form: hint contradicts the tenant’s input order

The booking-v3 date field prints MM/DD/YYYY. On a day-first tenant’s form, people follow that hint, while the form interprets the value in the tenant’s order. This is the hint failure, limited by the source classifier to the form channel on booking-v3. R-41230, from Copper Studio, is an example even though intake tagged it as parser. Correct the visible hint to match the actual parsing contract and test that contract for Alder Physio, Copper Studio and Pale Harbour. A parser-only change does not fix the misleading instruction on the form.
